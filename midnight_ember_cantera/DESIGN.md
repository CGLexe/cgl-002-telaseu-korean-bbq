## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the rare cross-pollination between high-end Korean culinary artistry and the centuries-old architectural gravitas of Oaxaca Centro Histórico. It balances nocturnal drama with heritage craft: the quiet, imposing mass of historic quarry stone (*cantera verde* / aged stucco) under midnight skies, pierced by the vivid hum of electric street signs and glowing tabletop charcoal pits (*charbon* / *yeontan*). 

The visual voice is mysterious, refined, unapologetic, and tactile. It eschews generic restaurant luxury in favor of an immersive sensory environment—invoking sizzle, smoke, oxidized bronze, and midnight neon.

### Design Movement
**Atmospheric Nocturne & Architectural Modernism:**
- **Surfaces:** Deep obsidian and soot canvases layered with warm, oxidized bronze and subtle fibrous hanji/linen grain.
- **Lighting:** Controlled radiance. Neon highlights are deployed sparingly like glowing glass signage in a dark colonial alleyway, avoiding chaotic neon-wash in favor of razor-sharp accents.
- **Typography:** Architectural tension between dramatic, editorial high-contrast serifs and ultra-precise modern grotesque sans-serifs.

### Emotional Target
The guest should feel as though they are stepping through a heavy 18th-century wooden gate into an intimate, neon-lit courtyard humming with charcoal heat, clinking bronze tableware, and world-class culinary curation.

## Colors

### Palette Rationale
- **Primary (`#F97316` — Ember Orange):** Direct homage to live oak charcoal sparks, flame-seared A5 wagyu, and the core neon filament of the iconic entrance sign. Used for primary calls-to-action, active reservation states, and focal highlights.
- **Secondary (`#38BDF8` — Midnight Blue Neon):** Derived from cold neon signage against dark colonial skies. Provides high-voltage contrast against embers; reserved for badges, status markers, cuts-of-the-day accents, and delicate nocturnal glints.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` — Yugi Bronze):** Reflects traditional Korean forged bronzeware (*Bangjja Yugi*), tabletop roaster rims, and colonial brass door pulls. Deployed for subtle borders, secondary luxury tags, prices, and ornate framing.
- **Neutral (`#0E1116` — Soot Obsidian):** A complex, ink-black neutral with cool slate undertones. Surface steps climb from `#0E1116` (deep void) up through `#14171F` (cantera surface) and `#1E232E` (elevated cards).

### Semantic & Functional Swatches
- **Surface Void:** `#0A0C10` (Background base)
- **Surface Primary:** `#0E1116` (Canvas layer)
- **Surface Elevated (Cantera/Tile):** `#14171F` (Cards, drawers)
- **Surface Highlight:** `#1F2430` (Interactive card states, table hover)
- **Border Subtle (Bronze Patina):** `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.22)`
- **Border Electric (Neon Cyan):** `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.40)`

- **Text Muted:** `#4B5563` (Charcoal grey)

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headlines & Display (`Playfair Display`):** Brings dramatic, high-contrast serif sophistication reminiscent of editorial menus, historic inscriptions, and classic vintage signboards. It commands space with italicized flourishes on key tasting descriptors and menu chapter titles.
- **Body & Technical Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its crisp geometry, open counters, and high legibility against pitch-black backgrounds. Prevents dark-mode optical halation and provides modern hospitality clarity across menu items, provenance notes, and booking workflows.

### Typographic Polish
- Set all uppercase category markers, banchan titles, and cut classifications using `label-sm` or `label-md` with `text-transform: uppercase` and tracking from `+0.06em` to `+0.08em`.
- For signature cocktails, mezcal selections, and prime cuts (e.g., *Hanwoo / Miyazaki Ribeye*), use `Playfair Display` in italicized weights within `headline-sm` to establish luxurious pacing.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The layout uses a 12-column desktop fluid grid (collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile). Margins are generous to evoke architectural grandeur, mirroring the open-air central courtyard (*patio central*) of an authentic Oaxacan casona.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Section padding defaults to `space-xl` scaled 2x (80px–120px) to give hero imagery and architectural photography generous breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 6 columns, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Dual-column layouts for grill sets and wine lists.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem margins, 1rem gutters. Sticky reservation bars anchored at bottom edges, single-column cascading course tastings.

### Spacing Cadence
Employ an 8pt base grid for micro-elements:
- Dense ingredient listings and side pairings use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Card interiors and dialog modals utilize `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px).
- Inter-section transitions use `space-xl` (40px) or multiples thereof.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an interplay of dark material density, fine metallic edges, and ambient neon back-glow rather than standard drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (The Street / Midnight Ground):** `#0A0C10` – Underlying bedrock background.
2. **Tier 1 (Architectural Cantera / Slabs):** `#0E1116` with a hair-thin 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
3. **Tier 2 (Elevated Plates / Interactive Modules):** `#14171F` with a 1px border of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)` (Warm Bronze Rim).

   - Subtle outer shadow: `0 20px 45px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`
   - Top highlight rim: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`

## Shapes

### Form Language: Chiseled Geometry
Reflecting hand-carved stone lintels, iron window gratings, and cast-iron grill plates, the system adopts a controlled, minimal corner radius (`roundedness: 1`). Soft curves (4px base) prevent visual harshness without degrading architectural authority.

- **Base Components (Buttons, Input Fields, Badges):** `rounded` (4px / 0.25rem).
- **Cards & Architectural Panels:** `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to ephemeral status dots and circular reservation time selectors (`50%` radius).
- **Dividers & Metal Filaments:** 1px hairline horizontal rules mimicking brass inlay strips found in polished terrazzo floors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Reserve Table" / "Order Experience"):** Flame Ember solid background (`#F97316`) with high-contrast obsidian typography (`#0A0C10`, weight 700), 4px radius. On hover: shifts toward vivid fiery red-orange (`#EA580C`) with a focused ember glow (`rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary ("Explore Cut & Cellar"):** Transparent container with a 1px Yugi Bronze rim (`#D97706`), warm gold typography (`#FBBF24`). On hover: fills with `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)` and illuminates the border.
- **Neon Electric Ghost ("Events / Omakase Bar"):** Hairline border in Midnight Cyan (`#38BDF8`), cyan-tinted glow on hover, crisp parchment text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Meat Marbling Grade / Cut Provenance (A5 Wagyu, Prime, Oaxacan Dry-Aged):** Compact badges with `#14171F` background, 1px border in ember or bronze, tracked uppercase typography (`label-sm`), punctuated with small circular embers.
- **Service Tags (Banchan Refills, Sommelier Selection):** Muted slate backdrops with cool blue-grey neon borders.

### Cards (Menu & Culinary Showcases)
- **Structure:** Solid `#14171F` base, 8px border radius, 1px perimeter border composed of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Image Integration:** Dark-vignetted food photography integrated seamlessly into the card top; bottom contains Playfair Display headings, weight/portion metadata in bronze, and subtle smoke-hued descriptions.
- **Hover Transition:** Surface lifts to `#1C212D`, the hairline border shifts to glowing Yugi bronze, and the image scales slightly (1.02x) behind an obsidian wash.

### Input Fields & Selectors (Reservation Flow)
- **Base State:** Background `#0E1116`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F3F4F6`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border transition to electric ember (`#F97316`) with a delicate flame-hued ambient halo. No generic browser outlines.
- **Guest / Date Counter:** Stepper buttons encased in chisel-cut square bronze-bordered segments.

### Lists & Menu Rosters
- **Format:** Minimal editorial ledger layout. Dish name left-aligned in `headline-sm` (Playfair Display), trailing dot leader or hairline bronze rule, accompanied by price right-aligned in sharp bronze sans-serif (`title-md`).
- **Tasting Menu Sub-items:** Nested under parent cuts with 16px left indentation, styled in `body-sm` muted smoke (`#9CA3AF`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Radio (Cut thickness, Dining room zone):** 18px square with 3px inner corner radius. Active state embeds an inner glowing square rendered in `#F97316`.
- **Checkbox (Dietary & Banquet options):** Dark obsidian square with a glowing bronze checkmark when active.
